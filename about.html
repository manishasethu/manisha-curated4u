A little about us

We make planning feel personal

Curated 4U is an event planning concept built around one simple idea: every celebration should feel like it belongs to the people celebrating.

Good events begin with good listening

We bring event ideas, service options and planning details into one simple journey. Whether you are organising a birthday, wedding, campus event or corporate gathering, you can begin with your needs and build from there.

Our goal is to make it easier to compare possibilities, understand approximate costs and communicate your requirements clearly.

  • Creative and flexible event concepts
  • Service suggestions based on event needs
  • Budget-conscious planning options
  • A clear enquiry and booking journey
